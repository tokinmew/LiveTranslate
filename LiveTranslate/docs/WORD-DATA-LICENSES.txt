Offline spoken-word data

The file backend/spoken-words.json.gz contains three separately licensed
collections of short source forms and English glosses. The adaptations below
retain the respective data licenses. The application code is separate.

Japanese

Jonathan Waller's Japanese Language Proficiency Test study vocabulary, Tanos
JLPT Resources, converted from Anki to JSON by contributors to
wkei/jlpt-vocab-api. Jonathan Waller permits reuse of the free content under
Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY). The data here were filtered, normalized
and indexed by unambiguous spelling and kana. English glosses were shortened.

https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/
https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/sharing/
https://github.com/wkei/jlpt-vocab-api/tree/c9e257663b2da005cf4f9a2477e80a9f108e4e26/data-source

Japanese expressions from JMdict

Live Translate uses the JMdict Japanese-English dictionary. Copyright in
this material belongs to James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary
Research and Development Group (EDRDG). The adaptation in
backend/ja-expressions.json.gz is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. The EDRDG documentation
and license are linked below.

The JMdict-simplified project by scriptin converted the dictionary into
JSON. This app uses the November 16, 2026 English snapshot. We selected
contemporary expressions, idioms, interjections and common discourse terms,
retained native spellings and unambiguous readings, selected a single
English gloss per form and compressed the resulting index.

https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified/releases/tag/3.6.2%2B20261116213322

Chinese

CC-CEDICT and its contributors, distributed by MDBG, building on CEDICT
by Paul Denisowski. CC-CEDICT data and the adapted Chinese glosses in this
collection are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International. The selection of HSK words comes from Complete HSK Vocabulary
by Yanis Zafirópulos and contributors, under the MIT license reproduced below.
Forms from levels 1 through 6 were extracted, filtered for usable glosses,
normalized, and indexed under simplified and traditional characters.

https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary/tree/7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9

Korean

CC Korean-English Dictionary by Masato Hagiwara and contributors, based on
Korean entries by English Wiktionary contributors. The dictionary and this
adapted collection of Korean entries are licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. Word forms and example translations
were selected, normalized, indexed and compressed. Glosses were shortened.

https://en.wiktionary.org/
https://github.com/mhagiwara/cc-kedict/tree/625a7556e7579d415727358d5b6e873aa5a50338
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
